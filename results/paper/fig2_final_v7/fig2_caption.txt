Figure 2. Structural prediction of thermodynamic, dynamic, and joint stability. (a) Joint precision-recall performance from the original single-task ALIGNN baseline to Full MTL and its final deep ensemble. ALIGNN and Full MTL single-model curves are pointwise means across the same three seeds (2025-2027); curve-level SD bands are omitted for visual clarity. The Full MTL ensemble curve is obtained by arithmetic averaging of five member probabilities (seeds 2025-2029). Legend AP values are computed separately from each seed's original predictions and summarized as mean +/- sample SD; ensemble AP is computed once from the averaged probabilities. (b) Joint AP progression for the same three stages. Error bars for ALIGNN and Full MTL denote sample SD across the three common single-model runs; the final five-member ensemble is one aggregate estimate and therefore has no artificial error bar. No statistical-significance claim is made across these different aggregation levels. (c) Joint AP of the Full MTL five-member ensemble under frozen Random (stratified random; N=1,215, 569 positives, 46.83%), Composition (canonical-composition-disjoint; N=1,211, 568 positives, 46.90%), Chemical system (element-set-disjoint; N=1,214, 566 positives, 46.62%), and Structure family (structure-family-key-disjoint; N=1,249, 589 positives, 47.16%) splits. The legacy IID set in panel a and the review Random split in panel c are distinct. (d) Joint AP and (e) probability-intersection-bound violation rate for Equal MTL, adaptive weighting without logic (+AW), fixed weights with logical consistency (+LC), and Full MTL. Colors and marker shapes identify the four variants; the compact legend in panel e applies to both panels. Each small translucent point is one single-model run for a common seed (2025-2027); large points and error bars show mean +/- sample SD. A violation in panel e occurs when pJ lies outside [max(0,pT+pD-1), min(pT,pD)] with epsilon=1e-10. Full MTL does not have the lowest mean violation rate; +LC is lower. (f) Error rates in four true stability classes for single-task ALIGNN and Full MTL, using each run's validation-selected Joint threshold. The first three classes report false-positive release rates and the T+/D+ class reports the false-negative rate; n labels give the number of distinct test materials per run. Full MTL has a higher mean false-positive rate than ALIGNN in the T+/D− class (0.399 versus 0.338), so the method does not reduce every error type. Panels d-f use three independent M=1 runs and show run-level sample SD.
