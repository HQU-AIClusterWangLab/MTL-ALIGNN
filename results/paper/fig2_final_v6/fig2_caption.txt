Figure 2. Structural prediction of thermodynamic, dynamic, and joint stability. (a) Joint precision-recall performance. ALIGNN and Equal MTL curves show the pointwise mean across five independent single-model runs (M=1), with shaded bands denoting the run-level sample SD; the Full MTL curve is obtained once by averaging the probabilities of five ensemble members (M=5). AP values are intentionally omitted from this panel. (b) Task-wise AP differences after matching all methods at M=5. Error bars are paired material-bootstrap percentile 95% confidence intervals (B=2,000; seed 260906). Both Thermo intervals and the Dynamic/Joint comparisons with ALIGNN cross zero; no universal significant improvement is inferred. Thus, panels a and b use different aggregation levels to answer framework-level and size-controlled questions, respectively. (c) Joint AP of the Full MTL five-member ensemble under frozen Random (stratified random; N=1,215, 569 positives, 46.83%), Composition (canonical-composition-disjoint; N=1,211, 568 positives, 46.90%), Chemical system (element-set-disjoint; N=1,214, 566 positives, 46.62%), and Structure family (structure-family-key-disjoint; N=1,249, 589 positives, 47.16%) splits. The legacy IID set in panel a and the review Random split in panel c are distinct. (d) Joint AP and (e) probability-intersection-bound violation rate for Equal MTL, adaptive weighting without logic (+AW), fixed weights with logical consistency (+LC), and Full MTL. Each small point is one single-model run for a common seed (2025-2027); large points and error bars show mean +/- sample SD. A violation in panel e occurs when pJ lies outside [max(0,pT+pD-1), min(pT,pD)] with epsilon=1e-10. Full MTL does not have the lowest mean violation rate; +LC is lower. (f) Error rates in four true stability classes for single-task ALIGNN and Full MTL, using each run's validation-selected Joint threshold. The first three classes report false-positive release rates and the T+/D+ class reports the false-negative rate; n labels give the number of distinct test materials per run. Full MTL has a higher mean false-positive rate than ALIGNN in the T+/D− class (0.399 versus 0.338), so the method does not reduce every error type. Panels d-f use three independent M=1 runs and show run-level sample SD.
